# Synopsis - Black/Matrix + (for translators and reviewers)

From a read of the whole script (2026-11-04, `batches.py story`), six chunks. Script numbers in brackets. Spoilers are the point: know what comes, don't give it away early.

## The world: morals are inverted
- The black-winged (黒き羽) rule; the white-winged (白き羽) are their slaves. Everyone worships Satan, 大善神 "the Great Good God", in the Angelus faith (アンゲルス教). God (ゴッド) is 大悪神 / 大邪神 "the Great Evil God".
- **Words of praise and blame are swapped, and the text means it.** 邪悪, 邪教, 罪深い are compliments ("wicked" is a good thing). 偽善 "hypocrisy" means kindness and love. The Seven Deadly Sins (七つの大罪) are freedom, justice, human rights, protecting the weak, friendship, equality and love. The Seven Virtues (七つの美徳) are wrath, pride, envy, sloth, gluttony, greed, lust. Translate them straight: the joke and the horror are that a priest says "you wicked, wonderful man" and means it. Love is the worst crime.
- The Genesis War (創世記戦争, 666 days): Mephistopheles' side won; the losers' seven archangels became the "Seven Great Evil Gods" (Michael, Gabriel, Raphael, Uriel, Sariel, Raguel, Metatron), sealed by the Chains of God (神の鎖). Their armors (大邪神の鎧) kill slowly and need the wearer's wings torn off. Seven chains hold the world; each armor unsealed snaps one.

## Story
- **Prologue [8-122].** Abel, a silent amnesiac white-winged slave, wakes from a ten-month fever in a house on a hill. The player chose one **master** (Domina, Purica, Kurreshes, Michette, Prague, or the secret male Zero): each scene exists once per master, in that master's voice. Abel lives with the master, does chores (mini-games), finds diaries [27-32]. He once went for herbs for the sick master and was found ringed by dead soldiers. Michette's diary calls him "Cain-oniichan" and accuses him of breaking a promise. Occult book [97-105]: the world's lore, with the morals as above. Inquisitors raid the house [70-75]: the master declares love, the worst crime, and is taken [121-122]. Abel is branded a Rebel Slave.
- **Golgotha Prison [126-143].** Cell: the Sacrifice Waiting Room. Cellmates Leburobus (huge ex-gladiator, wants freedom) and Gaius (cool thief, "Gai the Gale"), old Johannes (High Priest, necromancer), bullied guard Phillipo. Abel's touch heals Phillipo and turns his wings white: the sign of the Savior. Escape. Warden Prague (or Charon, on Prague's own route) joins for her sibling's sake (the master is her younger sister, or brother if Zero). Purica, caged meat thief, adopts Abel as her slave.
- **Ribeira [150-188].** Temple of the Sun. The armored Heretic Knight is Rupirupi, Johannes' apprentice, in a cursed armor that is killing her; she joins. Judas (camp high priest who ousted Johannes) stole the Metatron armor. Abel tears off his wings and wears it. Judas loses and points at Canaan and Pandemonium.
- **Pandemonium [203-225, 249-273].** Leburobus' past: gladiator-bred, parents killed in the arena. Bandit leader Oscar joins, later revealed a woman, Kurreshes-as-Oscar (クレージュ, her true name [225]). Zero ("Flash Zero") blinded Leburobus' eye and killed his wife; Zephar (a woman, slave hunter) killed his parents and framed him. Beaten; Zero joins in a truce. Ruler Mammon wears an armor and forces one on Phillipo's brother Parta, who dies. Leburobus (Uriel) and Phillipo (Sariel) take armors. Luca's Inquisition Crusade attacks.
- **Kederon [228-241].** Phillipo's village of old men: the Elder Death Squad (Mastema) stops the young from leaving. Domina (clinic nurse) and Michette (insists Abel is her Cain) join.
- **Canaan [284-320].** Baal-Zebul feeds slaves to guests and turns maids into vampires. Gaius takes Michael's armor to save them. He loses and regains his faith in justice.
- **Begild [327-362].** Judas, Mammon and Baal return. Marco (Raguel armor) and his adviser Cherubim rescue them; Begild is the base of the White-Wing Liberation Front. Xaphan, friend of the dead Seraphim, attacks. Abel is named the Savior.
- **Sodom and Gomorrah [367-402].** Capitalist "freedom" city (Stan) and planned-economy city (Belphe) each want the party to destroy the other. Refused either way; both burn at the Devil's Strait.
- **Moses and Noah [412-438].** Heretic Knight Moses tests them with illusions, leads them to Noah, the land of bondage, the Ark, and sunken Eden. He dies opening the Gate of the Underworld. Myth: God made apes in his image, Mephisto gave them the fruit of knowledge, God sent the Flood. Armor-wearers die young.
- **Underworld, Babel, Diabolos [444-475].** Fallen white-wings eat each other below the world. Luca is the great angel Lilith, and calls Johannes' body "a vessel". The party quarrels over trusting him. Ghost voices of angels. All are descended from fallen angels and apes.
- **Showdown [478-515].** Johannes denies being Mephisto. Cherubim, Judas, Baal, Astarte and Mammon are enemies (each a great angel: Judas=Beelzebub, Mammon=Behemoth, Baal=Moloch, Astarte=Belphegor, Cherubim=Alastor). The master lies dead on the altar. Rupirupi dies for Johannes, who is revealed as **Raphael** and wears her armor. The Pope is the Black Madonna, Japheth, Noah's daughter, an ape, wife of the long-dead Mephisto/Ha-Satan. Abel is Mephisto/Metatron reborn (heart transplant); the master is her child, and Satan's next bride or groom.
- **Choices and ending [524-557].** Abel may become God, become Satan, die, give up the master, or fight for an equal world. He eats the fruit and remembers brother Cain. The five villains speak through the Pope's body. The Pope, God and Satan are one being; Ha-Satan is a role, "the prosecutor". Gaius is Michael, Leburobus Uriel, Phillipo Sariel, Marco Raguel, Johannes Raphael reborn: each fights Abel and dies content. Endings: the second Adam or the second Eve.

## Placeholders
`{0020}` is Abel's name, `{0021}` the master's name, `{0023}` an item. Keep each exactly once where the Japanese has it. A name can be up to 12 letters, and an odd-length one leaves a half-letter gap before the next word: don't glue a suffix ("{0021}'s" reads fine, "{0021}-sama" reads fine, "{0021}ie" doesn't).

## Portraits
The `speaker` column comes from the portrait in the box (`tools/faces.json`, sheet in `work/qa/p5/portraits.png`). Blank: no portrait: narration, an off-screen voice, or a generic person; read the line. A `faceN` speaker has no name: judge from the text. Identifications are a reader's, so trust the text when it disagrees. A portrait with a body language variant (Johannes 5/29/30, Moses 53/121, Rupirupi 6/31/83) is the same person.
